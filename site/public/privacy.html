privacy policy

this page has never seen your search history. neither have we.

Last updated: November 2026 · applies to dayscriptplanner.org, the DayScript app (app.dayscriptplanner.org) and the DayScript planner PDFs.

DayScript is a planner. Your planner is yours. This page says exactly what we collect, why, where it lives, and how to take it back or make it disappear. It is written to be read, not scrolled past.

who we are

DayScript is a small, independent planner company based in New Jersey, USA, run by its founder. When this policy says “we,” it means DayScript. You can reach us at hello@dayscriptplanner.org. We are the “data controller” for the information described here.

what we collect, and why

the website

  • Waitlist email. If you join the app waitlist we store your email address, the time you joined, a one-word source tag (for example pdf or ig, so we know whether the QR code or Instagram sent you), and the browser type string. We use it to send you the app launch email and, at most, a handful of DayScript updates a year. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • No cookies, no trackers. The website sets no cookies and loads no analytics, ad pixels or third-party scripts. Fonts are served from our own domain.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) keeps standard, short-lived request logs (IP address, time, page) to run and protect the site. We don't build profiles from them.

the app

  • Account. To sync across devices you sign in with an email link or with Google or Apple. We store the email address and an account ID. If you use Google or Apple we receive only what they send for sign-in (your email and a name, if provided); we never see your password.
  • Your planner. The pages you write — typed text, handwriting strokes, to-dos, the margin line, settings — are stored so they can follow you from iPad to phone. Encrypted in transit and at rest. We also keep a short rolling history of snapshots so “undo to yesterday” works and nothing is ever lost.
  • Offline first. Until you sign in, everything stays on your device in the browser's local storage and nothing is sent to us. Signing in is what turns sync on.
  • Usage counts. For the AI features we record how many requests your account made each day (a number, not the content) so we can enforce fair-use limits and keep the lights on.
  • Error reports. If something breaks, the app may log a technical error (what failed, which screen, browser version). No page content is included.

the planner PDF

The PDF you buy on Etsy is a file. It phones no one. Your purchase is handled by Etsy under Etsy's privacy policy; we see your Etsy username and order details, nothing more. The QR code inside the PDF simply opens this website with a ?src=pdf tag.

the ai features

Some features read your page to help you — turning a handwritten list into to-dos, or drafting a day from your notes. When you use one, the text or an image of the ink from that page is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) through our own server, under their commercial terms, which do not permit them to train their models on your content. We don't keep a copy of what was sent beyond the request itself. These features run only when you tap them; nothing is read in the background.

where it lives

  • Website, waitlist and app hosting: Cloudflare (United States).
  • Accounts, planner data and snapshots: Supabase (database hosted in the United States, US East).
  • AI processing: Anthropic (United States).
  • Transactional email (sign-in links, launch email): our email provider, which receives only your address and the message.

Each provider is bound by a data-processing agreement and handles your data only on our instructions.

who sees it

Nobody, in the ordinary course of things. We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it with advertisers — ever. We share it only with the providers above so the product can work, or if the law genuinely requires it, in which case we'll tell you unless we're legally prevented from doing so. If DayScript is ever acquired, your data moves with the product under this same promise, and you'll be told first.

your controls

  • See it. In the app, Settings → “Export my planner” gives you everything as a file.
  • Fix it. It's a planner. Edit the page.
  • Delete it. Settings → “Delete my account” removes your account, planner, snapshots and usage counts within 30 days (backups roll off within 90). Until that button ships in the app, email us and we'll do it within 7 days and confirm.
  • Leave the waitlist. Click unsubscribe in any email, or write to us.
  • Ask. If you live somewhere with specific privacy rights (the EU/UK, California and other US states), you have them here too — access, correction, deletion, portability, objection — and we'll honor them without making you prove where you live. We don't discriminate against anyone for exercising them.

age

DayScript is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you think a child has made an account, tell us and we'll remove it.

security

Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS, with strict transport security). Planner data is protected by row-level security, which means the database itself refuses to show your pages to any account but yours. Our AI key never reaches your browser. We keep a published security page in our code repository and welcome reports at hello@dayscriptplanner.org.

changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app and by email before it takes effect, and the “last updated” line at the top will move. Small wording fixes just happen.
